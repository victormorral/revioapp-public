Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of our software application Revio created for iOS/Apple devices. We believe strongly in user privacy and data minimization. This document outlines exactly how your information is handled.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The App is designed to function entirely offline and operates locally on your device.

  • Personal Information: We do not collect, require, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
  • Usage Data & Diagnostics: The App does not track your behavior, usage patterns, or device analytics. No background tracking software or third-party analytic SDKs are integrated into the App.

2. Device Permissions & Notifications

The App may request permission to send you notifications. If granted, these notifications are used solely to deliver timely updates, reminders, or alerts within the App.

  • Local Processing: All notifications are scheduled and triggered locally on your device.
  • No External Servers: We do not use external push notification servers, tracking tokens, or remote databases to send these alerts.
  • Control: You can opt out or change your notification preferences at any time through your iOS device system settings.

3. Third-Party Access

Because we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third-party companies, advertising networks, or external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our App or app store guidelines. Any updates will be published directly to this page.
